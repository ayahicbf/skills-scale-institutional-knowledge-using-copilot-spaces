# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## OctoAcme Project Management Process (Summary)

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative project management approach:

- **Initiation:** Define project goals, stakeholders, and success criteria.
- **Planning:** Develop schedules, resource plans, role assignments, and deliverables.
- **Execution & Tracking:** Track progress with regular check-ins, adapt to changes.
- **Risks & Communication:** Identify risks, escalation paths, and maintain clear communication.
- **Release & Deployment:** Standardized procedures ensure quality on every release.
- **Retrospectives & Improvement:** Systematic process reviews drive ongoing improvement.
