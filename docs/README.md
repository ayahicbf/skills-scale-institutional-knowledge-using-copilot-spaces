# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## OctoAcme Project Management Process (Summary)

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative project management approach that ensures clear planning, effective execution, and continuous improvement across all projects. The process encompasses six key phases that work together to deliver quality results and maintain team alignment.

**Initiation & Planning:** Projects begin with clearly defined goals, identified stakeholders, and established success criteria. Teams develop comprehensive schedules, resource plans, role assignments, and detailed deliverables. This foundation ensures everyone understands what needs to be accomplished and how success will be measured.

**Execution & Tracking:** Once planning is complete, teams execute the project plan while maintaining visibility through regular check-ins and progress tracking. The team adapts to changes as they arise, ensuring the project stays on course. Risk identification and communication occur continuously throughout execution, with escalation paths defined for blockers and critical decisions. Clear stakeholder communication keeps all parties informed through regular status updates and incident notifications when needed.

**Quality & Deployment:** Before release, quality gates and acceptance criteria ensure deliverables meet standards. Standardized release and deployment procedures guarantee consistency and reliability on every release. Following deployment, retrospectives and process reviews drive ongoing improvement, capturing lessons learned and identifying opportunities to enhance future projects.
